# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-25

First release.

- **Quiz:** a Question key with four Answer keys. Plus and minus, times tables, division, mixed maths, missing letter and telling the time, at three levels, with two to four choices. A right answer turns green and earns a star on the first try; a wrong one shakes gently and says try again. Without Answer keys the Question key works as flash cards.
- **Spelling Bee**, **Word of the Day** (50 words, or your own) and **Fun Fact** (45 facts about space, animals, nature and the body), all read aloud.
- **Read to Me** reads copied text aloud; **Reading Timer** chimes and cheers at the end.
- **Star Chart** with a daily goal and trophy, and **Task Done** keys for daily routines.
- **Learning Site** opens eight kid-safe learning sites or your own, on screen 1, 2 or 3.
- Design System 4 from Windows Toolkit, unchanged: Glass (default), Vivid, Neon, Mono and Pro, with all the per-key look settings.
- Offline Windows voice, no accounts, nothing collected. A 15-key profile and a double-click installer that does not need Windows Toolkit.
